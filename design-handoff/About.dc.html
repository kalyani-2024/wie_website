About the chapter

We build the room we needed.

Mission

Women arrive in engineering in numbers. Far fewer of them stay.

Our chapter is the correction we could build ourselves: the skills the syllabus skips, mentors close enough to argue with, and a record of work that outlasts each graduating batch.

We are an affinity group of IEEE Women in Engineering, one of the largest international networks of women engineers. Every session we run is open to the whole campus, at any year of study.
