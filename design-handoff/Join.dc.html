Questions we get

Before you ask.

Do I have to be a woman to join?

No. Our focus is women in engineering, and our membership and sessions are open to every student on campus.

Do I need an IEEE membership?

Not to attend our sessions. An IEEE membership adds access to the wider network. [Confirm policy placeholder]

Which years can join?

Any. First years are especially welcome — most of what we run was built for the questions you have now.

How much time does it take?

As much as you want. Members attend what interests them; only council roles carry a commitment.
